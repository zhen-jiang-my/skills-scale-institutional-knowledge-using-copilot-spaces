# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Architect / Tech Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Architects and Tech Leads drive technical strategy, design system architecture, and ensure the implementation aligns with organizational standards. They provide technical guidance and mentorship while identifying and mitigating technical risks.

### Responsibilities
- Design system architecture and technical approach
- Review and approve technical proposals and implementation strategies
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Mentor developers on technical best practices
- Participate in technical design reviews and validation

### Decision Authority
- Final approval on technical approach and implementation strategy
- Technical trade-off decisions affecting system design
- Technology selection and architectural choices

### Interactions
- **Collaborates with Project Manager** on timeline feasibility and resource requirements
- **Reports technical risks to Risk Officer** for escalation and contingency planning
- **Interfaces with Quality Assurance** on testing strategy and technical validation criteria
- **Advises Business Analyst** on technical feasibility of requirements
- **Mentors Developers** on architecture and design patterns
- **Coordinates with Scrum Master** on technical impediments and process improvements

### Typical Communication
- Architecture design reviews and RFC (Request for Comment) documents
- Technical risk assessments and mitigation plans
- Code review guidance and technical decision documentation

---

## Change Management Officer

### Role Summary
Change Management Officers assess the impact of changes, develop communication and training strategies, and ensure successful stakeholder adoption. They bridge the gap between organizational change and end-user success.

### Responsibilities
- Assess change impact on processes, systems, and people
- Develop communication and training plans for changes
- Manage stakeholder adoption and resistance
- Create change readiness assessments
- Coordinate change rollout activities and monitor adoption metrics

### Decision Authority
- Approves change communication strategy and timing
- Approves training approach and materials
- Escalates adoption risks to Project Manager

### Interactions
- **Works with Project Manager** on change timeline and resource allocation for communication activities
- **Provides impact analysis to Risk Officer** for change-related risks and contingencies
- **Partners with Business Analyst** on requirement communication and stakeholder translation
- **Collaborates with Resource Manager** on training resource needs
- **Interfaces with Developers** on technical change details for stakeholder communication

### Typical Communication
- Change impact assessments and communication plans
- Training materials and stakeholder briefings
- Adoption metrics and readiness reports

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business needs and technical implementation by gathering requirements, analyzing stakeholder needs, and translating them for development and quality teams. They ensure solutions align with business objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements
- Validate stakeholder needs and priorities
- Translate requirements for development and QA teams
- Analyze gaps between current and desired states
- Create acceptance criteria and definition of done

### Decision Authority
- Requirement acceptance criteria and completeness
- Clarification of functional vs. non-functional requirements
- Change request impact assessment

### Interactions
- **Interfaces between Stakeholders and Development Team** as the requirements translator
- **Collaborates with Technical Architect** on technical feasibility of business requirements
- **Supports Change Management Officer** on requirement communication and training materials
- **Works with Quality Assurance** on test case validation and acceptance criteria
- **Advises Project Manager** on scope changes and requirement-related risks

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and user stories
- Acceptance criteria and scope definitions
- Requirement clarification sessions and impact assessments

---

## Risk Officer

### Role Summary
Risk Officers identify, assess, and track project risks. They coordinate mitigation strategies, ensure early escalation, and manage contingency planning to minimize project impact from identified risks.

### Responsibilities
- Identify and assess project risks across technical, organizational, and schedule dimensions
- Track risk mitigation status and effectiveness
- Report escalated risks to stakeholders
- Coordinate contingency planning activities
- Facilitate risk reviews and strategy adjustments

### Decision Authority
- Risk response strategy approval (mitigate, avoid, accept, transfer)
- Risk escalation to executive stakeholders
- Contingency plan activation

### Interactions
- **Reports to Project Manager** on risk status and escalations
- **Receives risk input from Technical Architect** on technical risks and dependencies
- **Receives risk input from Business Analyst** on requirements and scope risks
- **Collaborates with Change Management Officer** on adoption and organizational change risks
- **Coordinates with Resource Manager** on resource-related risks and dependencies

### Typical Communication
- Risk registers and assessments
- Mitigation plan updates and status reports
- Risk escalation notices and executive briefings

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate team ceremonies, remove blocking impediments, and coach teams on Agile practices. They optimize workflow and foster continuous process improvement within the team.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate Agile ceremonies (standup, planning, review, retrospective)
- Remove blocking impediments and escalate as needed
- Coach team on Agile practices and frameworks
- Optimize team workflow and processes
- Track and report team metrics and velocity

### Decision Authority
- Process and workflow improvements within team scope
- Retrospective action item prioritization and tracking
- Impediment resolution approach (escalation vs. resolution)

### Interactions
- **Supports Development Team** through ceremony facilitation and impediment removal
- **Reports impediments and metrics to Project Manager** for visibility and escalation
- **Coordinates with Technical Architect** on technical process improvements and blockers
- **Collaborates with Resource Manager** on team composition and skill development needs
- **Escalates organizational impediments** across teams

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and retrospective facilitation
- Velocity trends and team health metrics
- Impediment tracking and escalation notices

---

## Resource Manager

### Role Summary
Resource Managers own resource allocation, capacity planning, and skill development. They enable optimal utilization of team resources while supporting career development and organizational capabilities.

### Responsibilities
- Plan resource capacity and allocation across projects
- Track resource utilization and availability
- Coordinate onboarding and skill development
- Identify skill gaps and training needs
- Manage resource conflicts and competing priorities

### Decision Authority
- Resource assignment and allocation decisions
- Skill development investment and training approval
- Resource escalation and conflict resolution

### Interactions
- **Advises Project Manager** on resource capacity and availability for project planning
- **Supports line managers** on skill development and career growth planning
- **Collaborates with Technical Architect** on specialized technical skill needs
- **Works with Scrum Master** on team composition and cross-training opportunities
- **Partners with Change Management Officer** on training resource allocation

### Typical Communication
- Resource allocation plans and capacity forecasts
- Skill gap analysis and training plans
- Utilization reports and resource conflict resolution

---

## Compliance & Legal Advisor

### Role Summary
Compliance and Legal Advisors identify regulatory and legal requirements, ensure adherence to organizational policies, and manage compliance risk. They provide guidance on contractual and legal implications of project decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable compliance requirements and regulations
- Review contracts and legal agreements
- Assess legal and compliance risk
- Ensure adherence to organizational policies
- Provide guidance on legal implications of project decisions

### Decision Authority
- Compliance requirement approval and interpretation
- Legal risk mitigation strategy approval
- Contract terms and conditions acceptance

### Interactions
- **Works with Risk Officer** on compliance-related risks and regulatory requirements
- **Advises Project Manager** on compliance timeline impacts and legal constraints
- **Reviews requirements with Business Analyst** for compliance and contractual implications
- **Provides guidance to Technical Architect** on security, data protection, and compliance requirements
- **Escalates legal risks** to executive stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Compliance assessments and regulatory impact analyses
- Legal risk evaluations and mitigation strategies
- Contract reviews and approval documentation
